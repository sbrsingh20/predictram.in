Investment Advisor Agreement

This Investment Advisor Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into by and between:

Client: [Client Name], [Client Address]

Investment Advisor: [Advisor Name], [Firm Name]

Scope of Services

The Advisor agrees to provide investment advisory services to the Client, which include the following:

  • Asset allocation and portfolio management advice
  • Recommendations regarding the purchase, sale, or retention of securities
  • Ongoing monitoring and review of the Client's investment portfolio

Client's Responsibilities

The Client agrees to:

  • Provide accurate and timely financial information
  • Actively communicate with the Advisor about changes in their financial situation
  • Make the final decision on all investments

Fees and Compensation

The Client agrees to pay the Advisor a fee based on the agreed structure. Payment will be made based on the chosen terms (monthly, quarterly, or annually).
